Lead Qualification Tool for Service Businesses

Lead Qualification Tool helps service businesses collect, qualify, and organize incoming leads through a short WhatsApp chat.

The chat asks a few simple questions, identifies serious leads, and shows the business owner a clear dashboard with lead details, summaries, and follow-up information.

What it does

The tool helps businesses save time by filtering unqualified inquiries, collecting contact details, and organizing leads before a sales call or follow-up.
