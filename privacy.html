Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

Lead Qualification Bot helps businesses collect and organize basic lead information through a short chat flow. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that users provide during the chat, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Goal or request
  • Preferred start time or urgency
  • Email address, if a booking is requested
  • Chat messages sent through the system

How We Use Information

We use the information to:

  • Qualify and organize incoming leads
  • Show lead details to the business owner
  • Help the business follow up with interested users
  • Schedule appointments when requested
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the service

WhatsApp Communication

If users contact the business through WhatsApp or provide their phone number, the business may use that number to respond, follow up, or confirm appointment details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with the relevant business using the system, or with service providers needed to operate the system, such as hosting, messaging, or email providers.

Data Storage

Lead and booking information may be stored securely in our system so the business can review and manage inquiries. We take reasonable steps to protect stored information.
